Everything you need to know about charging an EV

Types of chargers, charging time, car companies, and tips to make every trip easier.

//charger types

Types of chargers

Home / wall socket

Slowest and cheapest. Good overnight for scooters, bikes and small cars.

AC charger (Type 2)

Found at homes, offices, malls and hotels. Good when you stay a few hours.

DC fast charger (CCS2)

Found on highways. Best for long trips, but check if your car supports it.

//steps

How to charge, step by step

1. Find a charger that fits your car (search by city or PIN code).

2. Check that the station is open and the charger is free.

3. Park close, switch the car off, and plug into the charging port.

4. Start the session with the app, QR code or RFID card.

5. Stop the session, unplug, and put the plug back neatly.

//tips

Handy tips

Charge 20% to 80%

Charging slows near full, so stopping at 80% saves time.

Plan your route

Check chargers on the way and keep a backup.

Carry your cable

Some AC stations need your own cable.

Be kind at stations

Move your car when done so the next person can charge.
